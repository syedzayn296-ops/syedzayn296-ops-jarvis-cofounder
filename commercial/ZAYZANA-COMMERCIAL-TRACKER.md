## Discovery Questions

1. Which operational processes still require the most manual work?
2. Where are staff moving information between spreadsheets, systems, WhatsApp, email or paper?
3. What information is difficult for management to get quickly enough to make a decision?
4. Where are recurring issues around stock, wastage, returns, customer service or administration?
5. Are there processes that work at individual stores but become difficult when managing multiple stores?
6. If you could eliminate one repetitive operational problem tomorrow, what would it be?
7. Who owns the problem and who approves a pilot or purchase?
